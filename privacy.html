Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Avalon.pk ("we", "us") provides WhatsApp ordering and order-management tools for restaurants. This policy explains what information is handled when you message a restaurant that uses our service.

Information we collect

When you place an order through WhatsApp we receive your WhatsApp phone number, your name, delivery address (for delivery orders), the items you order, payment method and order total.

How we use it

We use this information to process your order, show it to the restaurant, send you order updates, and, only if you have not opted out, send occasional follow-up messages from the restaurant.

Storage and sharing

Order data is stored on secure cloud services (Google Firebase) and processed through WhatsApp Business Platform by Meta. We share your order details only with the restaurant you ordered from. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can reply STOP at any time to stop follow-up messages. You can ask us to access or delete your data by contacting us below.
